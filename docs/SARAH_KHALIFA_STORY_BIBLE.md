# Sarah Khalifa — Story Bible v0.1

## Working thesis
The most compelling angle is not simply `celebrity accused in a drug case`. It is the contrast between public image, the scale of the case described by prosecutors, the legal process, and the fact that the case itself dramatically expanded public awareness of Sarah Khalifa.

## Working title
`سارة خليفة: القضية التي صنعت الشهرة`

Alternative:
`من الشاشة إلى حكم الإعدام: ملف سارة خليفة`

Use `حكم الإعدام` rather than implying a final/binding execution outcome when appellate review is relevant.

## Cold open
Open on **5 September 2026**, the Cairo Criminal Court ruling, then immediately establish that defense counsel announced an appeal. Rewind from that legal peak to reconstruct how the case reached court.

## Chapter map

### 1 — الحكم
Establish the ruling and the scale of defendants without dumping every detail.

### 2 — قبل أن تصبح القضية أكبر من الاسم
Describe the pre-case public/media image using verified background only. Avoid invented prestige claims.

### 3 — تغير اللعبة
Introduce the relationships/events that can be documented as relevant. Do not use gossip as connective tissue.

### 4 — الشبكة كما وصفتها النيابة
Visualize roles as allegations attributed to prosecution/referral records. Use an evidence graph; do not dramatize operational trafficking methods.

### 5 — أين بدأت الخيوط تظهر؟
Explain investigative chronology only from sourced reporting/records. The narrative function is `system starts to see the pattern`.

### 6 — الضبط والتحقيق
Use generic location/police/procedural visuals, source cards, maps, and document graphics. No fake CCTV/police bodycam.

### 7 — ملف الأدلة
Show categories: witnesses, technical evidence, digital material, photographs/video cited by prosecution. Do not fabricate the evidence itself.

### 8 — المحكمة
Timeline from referral/trial to the 5 September 2026 ruling; explain the distribution of outcomes and the defense announcement of appeal.

## Ending
End on legal uncertainty rather than sensational certainty:
`الحكم كان أكبر نقطة في القصة حتى الآن… لكنه ليس بالضرورة آخر نقطة فيها.`

Then display the exact `research verified through` date used for the final render.

## Visual direction
Investigative premium, not tabloid:
- black / charcoal / oxblood / archival amber;
- source labels and dates;
- maps and timeline lines;
- paper texture used sparingly;
- slow controlled camera language;
- faster montage only during escalation;
- silence/negative space around court outcomes.
