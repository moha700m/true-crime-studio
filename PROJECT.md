# Project Charter

## Product
A reusable Arabic true-crime / investigative documentary production system, with the Sarah Khalifa story as the first production.

## Audience
Arabic-speaking YouTube and short-form audiences who expect cinematic pacing, clear evidence, and modern visual storytelling.

## Commercial objective
Build a repeatable production pipeline rather than a one-off edit. Each completed film should leave reusable components: chapter openers, source cards, timelines, maps, evidence-board modules, captions, reconstruction labels, audio beds, thumbnail templates, and render presets.

## Success criteria
- Hook establishes stakes within the first 10 seconds.
- No unsupported factual assertion is presented as fact.
- Every reenactment is visually distinguishable from archival material.
- Visual state changes every ~5–20 seconds as appropriate, without random motion spam.
- Long-form master is optimized for YouTube 16:9.
- Structure can later generate 9:16 cutdowns without rebuilding the documentary from scratch.
- TypeScript typecheck passes before render.

## Non-goals
- Cloning the reference creator.
- Recreating a real person's face in fabricated criminal conduct and presenting it as authentic footage.
- Explaining operational drug-manufacturing, trafficking, evasion, or other actionable criminal techniques.
- Treating allegations, a first-instance judgment, an appeal announcement, and a final/binding judgment as equivalent.
